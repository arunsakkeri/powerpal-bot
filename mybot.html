Why I built PowerPal

I spent about four years working with Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, and the same beginner questions kept coming up. Is a flow part of Power Apps? What is Dataverse, and do I actually need it? Microsoft's documentation is thorough, but it's a lot to dig through when you just want to know where to start. PowerPal gives short, plain answers so beginners can figure out which tool fits their problem.

The Power Platform at a glance

Five tools sit on top of a shared data layer. PowerPal can explain each one and how they connect.
