Why I built PowerPal

When people first hear about Power Platform, the names blur together. Is a flow part of Power Apps? What is Dataverse, and do I need it? PowerPal gives short, plain answers to these questions so beginners can figure out which tool fits their problem and how to get started.

What PowerPal knows

PowerPal answers from a knowledge base I wrote that covers the core tools and common beginner questions.

Power Apps

Canvas apps vs. model-driven apps, Power Fx, and when to use each.

Power Automate

Automated, instant, and scheduled cloud flows, plus desktop flows (RPA).

Dataverse

Tables, relationships, business rules, and role-based security.

Power BI & Copilot Studio

Reports and dashboards, and Microsoft's own tool for building AI agents.

Environments & Solutions

How apps move from development to production, managed vs. unmanaged.

Learning Path

Free developer plan, Microsoft Learn, and certifications like PL-900.
